Background

Nature Conservation Management (NACOM) is the pioneer, non-government, pro-environment organization in Bangladesh, founded in 1987. Its mandates relate to natural resource management (NRM), climate change and resilience, biodiversity conservation, poverty alleviation, environmental and social assessment, capacity building, community mobilization for empowerment, and sustainable development through research, information exchange, and undertaking project and programs implementation. NACOM has highly experienced and multidisciplinary professionals to achieve its mandates.

Mission

NACOM's mission is to engage in activities to ensure the protection of nature while empowering local communities

Vision

"Protection of Nature for Better Human Life"

Objectives:

  • Advance Sustainable Resource Management: Engage diverse stakeholders to promote responsible natural resource use, biodiversity and wildlife conservation, ecosystem restoration, collaborative forest stewardship, and climate action.
  • Promote Applied Research and Policy Development: Expand knowledge to design effective strategies, plans, and policies for resource management, forest and wetland governance, ecosystem conservation, climate resilience, and the environment–development nexus.
  • Facilitate Knowledge Sharing and Innovation: Strengthen understanding of the climate crisis, conservation priorities, ecological processes, pollution control, and devise sustainable management tools through action-oriented research and exchange.
  • Implement Responsible and Inclusive Solutions: Support approaches that ease pressure on natural resources, foster sustainable economic growth, urban resilience, diversified livelihoods, and innovative climate adaptation and mitigation, while ensuring sound governance at all levels.
  • Promote Social Inclusion, Equity, and Justice: Empower vulnerable groups—including women, youth, and ethnic communities—to actively engage in conservation, resource management, environmental integrity, health, education, skills development, livelihood improvement, and protection of human rights.
  • Raise Awareness and Build Capacity: Equip policymakers, communities, youth, women, ethnic minorities, persons with disabilities, and the private sector to act on natural resource management, climate change, and sustainable development, ensuring environmental and social safeguards.
